**The problem.** Chartered accountants across India needed a reliable way to handle statutory tax filing and regulatory compliance — a domain where rules change often and errors are costly.

**What I did.** I designed the ICAI Tax Suite program end-to-end, including the onboarding process that brought over 30,000 chartered accountants onto the platform, and led pre-sales and strategic partnerships to support the rollout.

**What changed.** The platform became a trusted, widely adopted compliance tool — and the experience building rule-based pricing and compliance logic here is the same muscle I now bring to any domain with complex business rules.
