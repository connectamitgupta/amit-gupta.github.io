**The problem.** NACTUS needed to move beyond a single product into a broader platform serving both institutions (B2B) and individual learners (B2C), without fragmenting the engineering effort.

**What I did.** I led product strategy and execution across three connected product lines: WebRTC-based virtual classrooms, e-commerce for course sales, and performance-management tools, while managing technical partnerships and budget.

**What changed.** The platform scaled to serve 250+ educational institutions, with virtual classrooms and white-labelled ERP solutions becoming core, reusable capabilities rather than one-off builds.
