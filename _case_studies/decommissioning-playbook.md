**The problem.** Retiring an old service was a recurring source of last-minute fire drills — every team rediscovered the same risks (orphaned dependencies, missed stakeholder sign-off, no rollback plan) from scratch.

**What I did.** I documented a repeatable Service Decommissioning Playbook covering dependency checks, stakeholder sign-off, and rollback planning, and paired it with pre-sprint readiness checks so teams caught risks before a sprint started rather than during it.

**What changed.** The playbook was adopted as the official retirement standard. The pre-sprint readiness checks lifted sprint story completion above 90%, by catching problems before they became mid-sprint surprises.
