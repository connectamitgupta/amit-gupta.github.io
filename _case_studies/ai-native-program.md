**The problem.** Cisco's RunOn platform was moving from rules-based automation toward AI-native workflows, but there was no shared way to decide when an AI feature was actually good enough to ship — every team was evaluating "accuracy" differently.

**What I did.** Working across product, engineering, and data science, I helped define evaluation criteria spanning 14 distinct areas, each held to an 85%+ accuracy threshold before release. I coordinated integration planning across 5+ engineering teams so the multi-agent architecture (4+ domain agents) could be tested consistently, and delivered a demo of the resulting Eval Studio tooling to the MCP team.

**What changed.** Teams building AI features now have a shared, defensible bar for "ready to ship," rather than each team inventing its own definition of accuracy on the fly.
